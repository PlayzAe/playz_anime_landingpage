# The reader

![The manga home page](/screenshots/manga.png)

## Open a chapter

On a manga's page, the main button picks up where you are:

- **Start reading** if you haven't started.
- **Resume ch. 12 · p. 4** to go back to the chapter and page you were last on.
- **Read ch. 13** for your first unread chapter, when there's no saved place to resume.

Or click any chapter in the **Chapters** tab. **Find a chapter** searches by number or title, and **Newest first / Oldest first** flips the order.

## The top bar

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Back | Returns to the manga page (`Esc`) |
| Series name | Opens the manga page |
| Chapter menu | Jump to any chapter |
| **Scroll / Pages** | Reading mode (`M`) |
| **Right to left / Left to right** | Page direction, shown in page mode |
| **Fit width / Fit height** | How pages are sized |
| Previous / next chapter | Also `[` and `]` |

The bottom edge shows a progress bar and the page count, for example `12 / 40`.

The bars get out of the way while you read. They hide as you scroll down and come back when you scroll up, move the mouse to the top or bottom edge, or click the page in scroll mode.

## Scroll or pages

| Mode | How it reads | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Scroll** | All pages in one long strip | Manhwa, manhua, and anyone who likes scrolling |
| **Pages** | One page at a time | Traditional manga |

Press `M` to switch. The default is in **Settings**, **Reading**, **Reading mode**. Korean and Chinese titles always open in scroll mode; see [Manhwa and webtoons](/docs/reading/manhwa-and-webtoons).

## Page mode

- Click the left or right half of the page to turn it, or use the arrow keys, `Space`, `Page Down` and `Page Up`.
- **Right to left** is the default because Japanese manga reads that way. In right-to-left mode, clicking the left side or pressing `←` goes forward.
- Turning past the last page shows the end-of-chapter card, and turning once more opens the next chapter. Turning back from the first page opens the previous chapter.
- The next few pages load in the background so turning is instant (one page ahead with [data saver](/docs/settings/data-saver) on).

## Fit

- **Fit width** sizes pages to the width of the window. Good for scroll mode and tall pages.
- **Fit height** shows each whole page on screen. Good for page mode.

Your choice is remembered for every series.

## Progress and read chapters

- Your page is saved as you read, and the manga page offers to resume from it.
- A chapter counts as read once its last page has been on screen, and never in the first 5 seconds after opening it. Opening a chapter by accident doesn't mark it read.
- Mark chapters read or unread yourself with the check next to each one, or use **Mark all as read** and **Mark all as unread** in the **⋯** menu.
- Reading a title from **Plan to read** moves it to **Reading** in your library.

At the end of a chapter, a card offers **Next chapter**. On the newest chapter you'll see **You're caught up** instead.

If a chapter won't load, see [Read from another source](/docs/reading/read-from-another-source).
