# Manhwa and webtoons

Manhwa (Korean) and manhua (Chinese) are usually drawn as webtoons: one long vertical strip meant to be scrolled, not turned page by page. PlayzAnime reads them that way automatically.

## They always open in scroll mode

If AniList lists a title's country of origin as Korea or China, the reader opens it in **Scroll** mode, whatever your **Reading mode** setting says. Your setting still applies to Japanese manga.

You can still press `M` or use **Scroll / Pages** to switch. For a webtoon, that switch applies to the series you're reading right now and doesn't change your global setting.

> [!TIP]
> **Fit width** suits webtoons best. The strip fills the reader's width and you scroll straight down.

## Find manhwa and manhua

- The **Manga** home has a **Manhwa** shelf of Korean webtoons.
- **Discover**, with **Manga** selected, has an **Origin** filter: Japan (manga), Korea (manhwa) or China (manhua).

## Which sources to expect

Flame Comics and WeebCentral are the strongest sources for manhwa and manhua. New Korean series are often only on Flame Comics in English. With **Manga source** on **Auto**, PlayzAnime finds them for you. See [Sources and auto-pick](/docs/reading/sources-and-auto-pick).

## Downloaded manhwa

Downloaded chapters open in your global **Reading mode**, because the offline reader doesn't have the title's country of origin. If a downloaded manhwa opens in page mode, press `M`.
