# Data saver

Data saver is for mobile hotspots, capped plans and slow connections. It's one switch: **Settings**, **Network & privacy**, **Data saver**. It works the same in the Windows app and the web app.

## What it changes

| Where | Normal | With data saver |
|---|---|---|
| Streaming quality | The best the source offers | 720p at most |
| Video buffer | Loads well ahead of where you are | Keeps a small buffer, so less is wasted if you stop |
| Next episode | Prepared in the background once you're past about halfway | Not prepared until you press **Next** |
| Manga pages | Full-size pages | Compressed pages where the source offers them (MangaDex does), often a third of the size |
| Reader | The next few pages load ahead | One page ahead |

Downloads from MangaDex also use the compressed pages while it's on. Episode downloads keep their own setting: see [Quality](/docs/downloads/quality).

## What it doesn't change

- Your library, history and profile. They're tiny and stay on your device.
- Downloaded episodes and chapters. They play from your disk, not the internet.
- Artwork in lists. Covers are already small.

> [!TIP]
> Watching on a hotspot often? Download episodes on Wi-Fi first and watch them later with no data at all. See [Offline mode](/docs/offline/offline-mode).
