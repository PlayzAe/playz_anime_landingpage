# Your profile

Your profile is how you show up when you share your lists with friends. There's no account and no sign-in: it's saved on your computer (in the web app, in your browser) and goes nowhere unless you export it.

## Open it

Click your picture at the bottom of the left rail, or open **Profiles**. The top card shows your name and picture with a few numbers: titles in your library, shows watched, series read and favourites.

## Edit it

Click **Edit profile**:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Required. What friends see. |
| **Picture** | Any image from your computer. It's resized and stored with the profile. |
| **About you** | One optional line, like "Seinen first, then whatever's airing". |
| **Favourites** | Pick from titles you've saved or watched. On a fresh install this list starts empty. |

Your changes are kept as soon as you confirm them.

## Skipped it at first launch?

That's fine: open **Profiles** and click **Edit profile** whenever you like. PlayzAnime works the same without one.
