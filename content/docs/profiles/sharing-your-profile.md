# Share your profile

Sharing works like sending a photo: you save your profile as one small file and send it however you like. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Send yours

1. Open **Profiles** and click **Share as file**.
2. Save the `.playzanime` file.
3. Send it to a friend: Discord, email, a USB stick, anything.

The file holds your name, picture, the line about you, your favourites, your library lists, and what you've watched and read. It doesn't include your settings, your downloads or anything else on your computer.

## Open a friend's

Any of these works:

- On **Profiles**, click **Import a profile** and choose the file.
- Drag the file onto the PlayzAnime window.
- Double-click the file (Windows app, installer version).

Their profile appears under **From friends**. Open it to see their favourites and lists in its own tab, and add any of their picks to your own library in one click.

To see a friend's newer lists, ask them for a fresh file and import it.

> [!NOTE]
> A profile file only carries lists and a picture. PlayzAnime checks every file it imports and refuses anything that isn't a PlayzAnime profile.
