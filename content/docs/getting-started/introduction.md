# Introduction

PlayzAnime is a free app for watching and downloading anime and for reading and downloading manga, manhwa and manhua. It has no ads and no accounts.

It comes in two forms:

- **The Windows app**, for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). It can download episodes and chapters and play them with no internet.
- **The web app**, the same app in your browser. It streams and reads, but it can't download. See [PlayzAnime on the web](/docs/web-app/about-the-web-app).

![The PlayzAnime home screen](/screenshots/home.png)

## Where things come from

PlayzAnime doesn't host any video or manga pages. It finds them on public third-party sites and plays or shows them in its own player and reader.

| What | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Titles, artwork, descriptions, airing schedule | AniList |
| Episode stills that AniList doesn't have | Kitsu |
| Episodes | A third-party episode source and the video hosts it uses |
| Chapters | MangaDex, WeebCentral, Flame Comics and MangaPill |

> You're responsible for how you use PlayzAnime and for following the laws where you live. Read the [disclaimer](/docs/policies/disclaimer).

## What you can do

- **Watch** in PlayzAnime's own player: skip intro, up next, subtitles in any script, keyboard shortcuts, picture-in-picture, and Windows media keys.
- **Read** in a reader built for both page-by-page manga and long-strip manhwa. Four chapter sources are checked for every title and the best one is picked for you.
- **Download** episodes as MP4 with subtitles inside, and chapters as CBZ, into tidy folders you choose.
- **Go offline.** With no internet, PlayzAnime opens on your downloads, and they play and read inside the app.
- **Keep lists** of what you're watching, reading, planning, finished, paused or dropped. They're stored on your computer.
- **Share a profile** as a small `.playzanime` file so friends can see what you watch and read.
