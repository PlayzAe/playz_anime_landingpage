# Search, library and schedule

## Search

Press `Ctrl K` or `/` anywhere, or click the search box at the top. Type at least two letters.

Results come from AniList: up to six anime and four manga, plus any matching pages of the app. Use `↑` and `↓` to move, `Enter` to open, and `Ctrl Enter` to see every result in Discover. `Esc` closes search.

Before you type, search shows **Pick up where you left off** with your latest episodes and chapters.

Common short names work. Typing `jjk`, `aot`, `fmab` or `sxf` searches for the full title, and search shows what it looked for under the box.

> [!TIP]
> If nothing comes up, try the romaji title. AniList knows most titles by both names.

## Discover

Discover browses everything on AniList with filters. Switch between **Anime** and **Manga**, then filter by:

- **Sort:** Trending, Most popular, Highest rated, Newest (or Best match while searching)
- **Genres:** click as many genre chips as you like
- **Format:** TV series, Movies, ONA, OVA, Specials, TV shorts; or Manga and One-shots
- **Origin (manga):** Japan (manga), Korea (manhwa), China (manhua)
- **Status:** Airing / ongoing, Finished, Not yet released
- **Season** and **Year** (anime)

**Clear** resets every filter.

## Library

Open any show or manga and use **Add to library** to put it on a list. The button then shows the list it's on, and the same menu moves it or removes it.

| List | Anime name | Manga name |
|---|---|---|
| watching | Watching | Reading |
| planning | Plan to watch | Plan to read |
| completed | Completed | Completed |
| paused | Paused | Paused |
| dropped | Dropped | Dropped |

Two things happen on their own:

- Starting a title from **Plan to watch** or **Plan to read** moves it to **Watching** or **Reading**.
- Every episode you watch and chapter you read is recorded in **History**.

The Library page filters by **Everything**, **Anime** or **Manga** and has a tab per list plus **History**. History keeps your 80 most recent shows and 80 most recent series.

> [!NOTE]
> Your library lives on your computer (or in your browser on the web app). There's no account and no sync. To show friends your lists, [share your profile](/docs/profiles/sharing-your-profile).

## Schedule

Schedule shows one week of airing episodes, with times in your time zone. Switch between **Everything airing** and **My library** to see only shows you've saved. Use the arrows to move a week at a time and **This week** to come back.

Shows that haven't started yet say **Not out yet** on their page. Add one to your library and it appears in your schedule once it airs.

## Show and manga pages

Each title's page has:

- A main button: **Watch E1**, **Resume E5**, **Start reading** or **Resume ch. 12 · p. 4**.
- **Add to library**, and **Trailer** when AniList has one.
- Tabs for **Episodes** or **Chapters**, **Related**, and **More like this**.
