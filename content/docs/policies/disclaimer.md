# Disclaimer

PlayzAnime is a free, open-source viewer for anime and manga. It **does not host, upload, store or distribute any media**.

- **Titles, artwork, descriptions and schedules** come from [AniList](https://anilist.co)'s public API.
- **Episodes and manga pages** are published by third-party websites. PlayzAnime shows them the way a browser would.
  - The Windows app opens them from your computer.
  - The web app's server forwards requests to them as they happen and stores nothing.
- **Downloads** made with the Windows app are saved only on your own computer, by you. PlayzAnime never sees or shares them.

PlayzAnime isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to AniList, any streaming service, publisher, studio or rights holder. All trademarks, names and artwork belong to their respective owners.

You're responsible for how you use PlayzAnime and for following the laws where you live. If you own content and want it disabled, see [Copyright & DMCA](/docs/policies/copyright-and-dmca). We act on valid notices within 72 hours.

PlayzAnime is provided "as is", without any warranty. See the [Terms of Service](/docs/policies/terms-of-service).
