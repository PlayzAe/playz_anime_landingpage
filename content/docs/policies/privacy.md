# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 26, 2026*

The short version: **PlayzAnime has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.** Your lists, history and profile stay on your device.

## What stays on your device

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Windows app | Settings, library, watch and reading history, your profile, download records. Kept in `%APPDATA%\PlayzAnime` (the portable app keeps them in the `PlayzAnime Data` folder beside it). |
| Web app | The same, kept in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes it. |

Nothing above is sent to us. We don't have a server that stores users or their data.

## Profiles you share

A `.playzanime` profile file contains your chosen name, picture, favourites, library and history. It's shared only when **you** send the file to someone. Anyone who has the file can read it.

## Who PlayzAnime talks to

To work, PlayzAnime makes requests to:

- **AniList** (anilist.co) for titles, artwork and schedules.
- **Kitsu** (kitsu.app) for episode thumbnails.
- **The episode and manga sources** that publish the content you open.
- **GitHub**, only when you click a link to it, or when the Windows app fetches ffmpeg the first time you download an episode.

These services see your IP address and the requests your device makes, as with any website, and their own privacy policies apply.

**On the web app,** requests to sources go through the web app's server. It forwards them as they happen and doesn't store them. It may keep short technical logs (the request path and any errors, not your identity) for up to 7 days to fix problems.

## Children

PlayzAnime isn't directed at children under 13. Adult titles are hidden by default.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
