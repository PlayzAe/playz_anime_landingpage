# Copyright & DMCA

*Last updated: September 26, 2026*

PlayzAnime respects the rights of creators and publishers, and we act on valid copyright notices quickly.

PlayzAnime doesn't host video, images or manga pages. It shows content that third-party websites already publish, and the web app's server only forwards requests to them as they happen, keeping nothing. That means **the content itself stays on those sources**. For it to come down, send a notice to the website that hosts it too.

## What we do when we get a valid notice

Within 72 hours of a complete notice, we:

1. **Disable the title in the PlayzAnime web app.** Its episodes and chapters stop loading for everyone, and the web server refuses requests for it.
2. **Remove any link to it** from our websites and documentation.
3. **Block the named source address** from the web app's server, when the notice identifies one.
4. Reply to confirm what we did.

We keep a record of notices, and we don't restore disabled titles unless a valid counter-notice requires it.

## How to send a notice

Open an issue at [github.com/PlayzAe](https://github.com/PlayzAe) titled **"Copyright notice"**, or use the contact address listed there. Include:

1. Your name, company (if any), and a way to reach you.
2. The copyrighted work you own or are authorised to represent.
3. Exactly where it appears in PlayzAnime: the web app address (for example `/anime/12345`) or the title's name, and the episode or chapter.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised by you, your agent or the law.
5. A statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

Incomplete notices may take longer, because we'll need to ask for what's missing.

## Counter-notices

If you believe a title was disabled by mistake, you can send a counter-notice with:

1. Your name and a way to reach you.
2. What was disabled and where it appeared.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith it was disabled by mistake or misidentification.
4. Consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate court and to accept service from the person who sent the original notice.
5. Your physical or electronic signature.

## Repeat infringers

We block, from the web app and its server, anyone who repeatedly uses PlayzAnime to infringe copyright.

## Misuse

Knowingly false notices or counter-notices can make the sender liable for damages. If you're unsure whether something infringes your rights, please get legal advice first.
