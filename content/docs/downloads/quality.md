# Quality

## Download quality

There are two places to set it:

- **Settings**, **Downloads**, **Episode quality** is the default for new downloads. It starts at **Best available**.
- The **Quality** choice in the download dialog overrides it for that batch.

| Choice | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Best** | The largest size the source offers |
| **1080p** | 1080p, or the closest lower size |
| **720p** | 720p, or the closest lower size |
| **480p · smallest** | 480p, or the closest lower size |

If the source has nothing at or below your choice, the smallest size it has is used. The size you actually got is in the file name and on the Downloads page.

## Disk space

The download dialog estimates the total before you start. For a 24-minute episode:

| Quality | About |
|---|---|
| Best or 1080p | 420 MB |
| 720p | 260 MB |
| 480p | 150 MB |

A 12-episode season at 720p is about 3 GB. A movie is roughly four episodes' worth. These are estimates; real sizes depend on the source.

Chapters are small by comparison: a CBZ is the pages exactly as the source serves them.

## Streaming quality

Streaming is separate from downloads:

- The player picks a quality automatically to suit your connection (**Auto quality**).
- The gear button in the player lets you fix a size for the episode you're watching.
- [Data saver](/docs/settings/data-saver) caps streaming at 720p.

Data saver doesn't change download quality. If you're on a capped plan, choose **720p** or **480p** for downloads too.
