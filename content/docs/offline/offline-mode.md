# Offline mode

Everything you've downloaded plays and reads inside the Windows app with no internet. There's nothing to switch on.

> [!NOTE]
> Offline mode is part of the Windows app. The web app needs a connection. See [PlayzAnime on the web](/docs/web-app/about-the-web-app).

## How it works

- With no connection, PlayzAnime opens on the **Downloads** page, the one part that works offline.
- **Play** on an episode opens it in PlayzAnime's own player, with its subtitles. Seeking works as usual.
- **Read** on a chapter opens it in the reader. Next and previous move between the chapters of that series you've downloaded.
- Your progress is saved as usual and picks up where you stopped when you're back online.

When the connection returns, PlayzAnime says so and everything else works again. The page you're on stays put.

## What's different offline

- **No skip intro.** Downloaded episodes don't carry the intro markers. See [Skip intro and up next](/docs/watching/skip-intro-and-up-next).
- **Up next** still works between downloaded episodes of the same show.
- Search, Discover, the schedule and new episodes need the internet.

## Get ready for a trip

1. On Wi-Fi, open a series and click **Download** for the episodes or chapters you want.
2. Wait until each one shows as finished on the **Downloads** page.
3. That's it: they're on your computer. See [Downloading episodes](/docs/downloads/downloading-episodes) and [Downloading chapters](/docs/downloads/downloading-chapters).

Your downloads are ordinary files, so they also open in other apps. See [Open downloads in other apps](/docs/offline/other-apps).
