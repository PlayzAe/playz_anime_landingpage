# A stream won't load

Episodes come from third-party hosts, and they have bad moments. Most problems fix themselves with one of these steps. Try them in order.

## 1. Try again

When a stream fails, the player shows **This stream wouldn't load** with **Try again**. Click it once: PlayzAnime asks the host for a fresh link, which fixes most one-off failures.

## 2. Switch to Sub or Dub

Below the player, switch between **Sub** and **Dub**. They often come from different servers, so one can work when the other doesn't.

## 3. Use the embed player

Click **Use the embed player** (or choose **Embed** next to Sub and Dub). It plays the host's own player inside PlayzAnime. It has fewer features, but it takes a different route to the video. See [The embed player](/docs/watching/embed-player).

## 4. Check the basics

- **Is it every episode or just one?** If only one, the host may not have it yet. Try again later.
- **Your connection.** Open a page in your browser. On a slow connection, turn on [data saver](/docs/settings/data-saver) for smaller streams.
- **VPN or DNS blockers.** Some block the video hosts. Pause them and try again.

## 5. Wait a little

If nothing works for any episode, the host is probably down. It usually comes back within hours, and PlayzAnime uses it again automatically.

## Other reasons

- **You're offline.** PlayzAnime says so when the connection drops. Downloaded episodes still play: see [Offline mode](/docs/offline/offline-mode).
- **The title was removed** at its rights holder's request. It won't play from any source. See [Copyright & DMCA](/docs/policies/copyright-and-dmca).

## Still stuck?

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/PlayzAe/playz_anime_desktopapp/issues) with the show, the episode and what you tried. A screenshot helps.
