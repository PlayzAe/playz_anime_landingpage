# Embed player fallback

PlayzAnime has two players:

| Player | What it is |
|---|---|
| **PlayzAnime** (default) | PlayzAnime's own player. Keyboard shortcuts, skip intro, up next, subtitle choice, no ads. |
| **Embed** | The episode source's own player, shown inside PlayzAnime. Kept as a fallback. |

## When to switch

Use the embed player when PlayzAnime's player can't get the stream:

- The player stays on **Finding the stream** and then says **This source is slow today. Still trying…**
- You see **This stream wouldn't load** even after **Try again**.

Both screens have a **Use the embed player** button. You can also switch with **PlayzAnime / Embed** under the player.

## Switch for one episode or for good

- The switch under the player applies to the episode you're watching.
- To make the embed player the default, go to **Settings**, **Watching**, **Player** and choose **Embed**.

## What's different in the embed player

The embed player belongs to the source, so PlayzAnime's player features don't apply inside it: no PlayzAnime shortcuts, skip intro button, up next card or subtitle picker. Use the controls the embedded player shows.

Your place is still kept as far as possible. The embed player opens at your saved position, and PlayzAnime saves your progress whenever the embedded player reports it.

## Ads and pop-ups

Source players often try to open pop-ups or redirect to ads.

- **Pop-up windows are always blocked**, and the embedded page can't navigate PlayzAnime away.
- **Settings**, **Network & privacy**, **Block ads and pop-ups** filters ad and tracker requests inside embedded players. It's on by default.
