# Subtitles and audio

## Sub or dub

When a show has an English dub, a **Sub / Dub** switch appears on its page and under the player. **Dub** is greyed out on episodes that have no dub.

PlayzAnime decides which one to start with in this order:

1. **What you last used for this show.** If you watched episode 4 dubbed, episode 5 opens dubbed too.
2. **Your global preference**, **Settings**, **Watching**, **Prefer English dub**.

Switching Sub or Dub on a show also updates that global preference, so new shows follow your latest choice.

> [!NOTE]
> Each show remembers its own audio. You can watch one show dubbed and another subbed without changing settings back and forth.

## Subtitle tracks

When you watch subbed, PlayzAnime turns on a subtitle track automatically. It picks:

1. A track in your **Subtitle language** (**Settings**, **Watching**).
2. If there isn't one, the track the source marks as its default.

The language list is English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Arabic and Indonesian. What's actually offered depends on the source for each episode.

When you watch dubbed, subtitles start off. Turn them on from the subtitles button or with `C`.

To change track while watching:

- Click the **Subtitles** button and pick a track or **Off**.
- Press `C` to cycle through the tracks and then **Off**.

## Scripts and fonts

Subtitles in Arabic, Hebrew, Chinese, Japanese and Korean display correctly. Each subtitle line takes its reading direction from its own text, so right-to-left lines read right to left, and the font falls back to your system's fonts for those scripts.

## Subtitles in downloads

When you download a subbed episode, the subtitles in your **Subtitle language** are packed into the MP4 as a subtitle track. Other players show it as a track you can turn on, and PlayzAnime's player shows it when you play the download offline. See [Downloading episodes](/docs/downloads/downloading-episodes).
