# Skip intro and up next

Both features depend on the source marking where the opening and the credits are. Most episodes have these markers; some don't.

## Skip intro

While the opening plays, a **Skip intro** button appears in the player. Click it or press `S` to jump to the end of the opening.

To skip every marked opening without clicking, turn on **Settings**, **Watching**, **Skip intros automatically**. The player shows **Skipped intro** each time it does it. It's off by default.

## Up next

When the credits start and there's another episode, PlayzAnime offers it:

- With **Autoplay** on, an **Up next** card counts down from 10 (**Playing in 10**). Choose **Play now** to go straight away or **Cancel** to stay. The countdown pauses while the video is paused.
- If the source didn't mark the credits, the card appears when the episode ends, with a 5-second countdown.
- With **Autoplay** off, the card appears at the end without a countdown, and a **Next episode** button shows during the credits.

Turn autoplay on or off with the **Autoplay** switch above the episode list, or in **Settings**, **Watching**, **Autoplay next episode**. It's on by default.

You can go to the next or previous episode at any time with `N` and `P`.

## Offline

Downloaded episodes don't carry intro or credit markers, so there's no skip intro offline. Up next still works between downloaded episodes of the same show, using the end of the file. See [Offline mode](/docs/offline/offline-mode).
