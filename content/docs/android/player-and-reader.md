# Player and reader on a phone

## Watching

Tap an episode and the phone turns to fullscreen by itself. The exit button (bottom right) brings you back to the episode list in portrait. Switch auto-rotate off in **Settings → Watching** if you prefer to start in portrait.

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Tap | Show or hide the controls |
| Double-tap left or right | Back or forward 5 seconds (change it in Settings) |
| Double-tap the middle | Play or pause |
| Swipe up or down on the left | Brightness |
| Swipe up or down on the right | Volume |
| Swipe sideways | Scrub through the episode |
| Hold | Play at 2× while held |

The lock button stops pocket taps. The picture-in-picture button, or leaving the app mid-episode, keeps the video playing in a small window. While an episode plays, the notification shade shows play, pause and next.

## The playback sheet

The settings button in the player opens a short sheet:

- **Quality**: every quality the source sends. Some new releases arrive in only one quality at first; the sheet says so.
- **Subtitles** and **Subtitle style**: size, colour, background box, outline and position, with a live preview. The same style is in **Settings → Watching**.
- **Audio**: Japanese with subtitles or the English dub, when there is one.
- **Speed**, **Picture** (fit, zoom or stretch) and **Player**.

**Player** chooses between PlayzAnime's player and the source's own web player. If an episode won't start, the error offers the source player; a bar at the top always takes you back.

If a title isn't on the streaming source at all, PlayzAnime says **Not on the source yet** instead of spinning. Niche and Chinese releases are often added late.

## Reading

The settings button in the reader opens the reading modes:

- **Paged (left to right)** and **Paged (right to left)**: tap the edges or swipe. Right-to-left puts page 1 on the right, like a printed manga.
- **Paged (vertical)**: swipe up for the next page.
- **Long strip** and **Long strip with gaps**: for manhwa and webtoons. **Page width** adds side margins on wide screens and foldables.

In paged modes, **Fit width** fills the screen side to side and scrolls tall pages; **Fit screen** shows the whole page. Pinch to zoom in either.
