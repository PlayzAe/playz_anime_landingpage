# Downloads on Android

## Downloading episodes

Tap the download icon on an episode, or hold one episode and tap others to select several. The download sheet lets you choose:

- **Episodes**: a range from one episode to another, with *Next 5*, *Unwatched* and *All* shortcuts.
- **Audio**: Japanese with subtitles, or the English dub.
- **Quality**: 1080p, 720p, 480p or 360p, each with its approximate size. If an episode doesn't have that quality, the nearest one below is used.
- **Subtitles**: English, all languages, or none. Your subtitle style is used when you watch the download in PlayzAnime.
- **Saving to**: your anime folder, which you can change right there.

![The download sheet: range, audio, quality and subtitles](/screenshots/mobile/download.webp "phone")

For manga, the chapter list's **Download…** offers the next ten, everything unread, or all chapters. Chapters from extensions download through the extension.

## While it downloads

- Progress shows in the notification shade, and downloads keep going with the screen off.
- The download button in the top bar shows how many are running. It opens **Downloads**.
- Pause, resume or cancel any download, or **Pause all**.
- If the connection drops, downloads wait and carry on when it comes back. Stream links that expire mid-download are renewed automatically.
- Closing the app or restarting the phone doesn't lose anything: unfinished downloads pick up where they stopped next time you open PlayzAnime.

## The Downloads page

Everything on your phone, grouped by show, filtered *Everything*, *Anime*, *Manga*, *Manhwa* or *Manhua*. Tap a cover to play an episode, read a chapter or delete them.

![Finished downloads, saved on the phone](/screenshots/mobile/downloads-done.webp "phone")

## Offline

With no connection, PlayzAnime opens on your downloads and shows an offline banner with a shortcut to them. Downloaded episodes play and downloaded chapters read exactly as they do online.

## Only on wifi

**Settings → Downloads → Only download on wifi** holds the queue on mobile data and resumes on wifi.
